About me

From the Upper Blue Nile
to the Central Valley.

My work is grounded in a simple question: how can a better understanding of water lead to better decisions?

I’m Berhanu Geremew Sinshaw, a Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in Environmental Systems at the University of California, Merced. I work in the Forest Ecohydrology & Watershed Systems (FEWS) Lab, co-advised by Professor Safeeq Khan and Professor Joshua Viers.

My expertise lies in integrated hydrological modeling, climate change impacts on water systems, and sustainable groundwater management in arid and semi-arid regions.
