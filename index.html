Welcome

I am a PhD candidate and graduate research assistant at the University of California, Merced, working in the Forest Ecohydrology & Watershed Systems (FEWS) Lab under the supervision of Professor Safeeq Khan and Professor Joshua Viers. My work examines hydrological processes and water-resource challenges in regions facing increasing climate stress.

My research focuses on modeling regional surface water and groundwater systems under a changing climate. I combine numerical modeling and analytical methods to investigate hydrological interactions and inform strategies for sustainable water management.

Research Interests

  • Surface water–groundwater interactions
  • Integrated hydrological and watershed modeling
  • Groundwater pumping and streamflow depletion
  • Climate impacts on regional water availability
